Universal Amendment to the Standard Contract for Microsoft's Commercial Marketplace

Version 1.0 · Publisher: SP Additions Limited, 27 Old Gloucester Street, London, WC1N 3AF, United Kingdom · Amends the Standard Contract for Microsoft's commercial marketplace

This Amendment forms part of and modifies the Standard Contract between SP Additions Limited ("Publisher") and Customer for the "Read & Understood Compliance Suite" offering (the "Offering"), comprising the applications identified in the offer listing. Capitalised terms are as defined in the Standard Contract. In case of conflict, this Amendment prevails.

1. Metered Fees. Fees comprise a recurring base fee plus usage-based fees as set out in the Order. Usage is measured by the Offering and reported to Microsoft for billing. Publisher's measurement records are deemed accurate unless Customer demonstrates manifest error. Accrued usage fees are non-refundable and, on termination, remain payable for usage up to the termination date.

2. Free Trial. Free trials are limited to one per Microsoft 365 tenant. If Customer does not purchase a paid subscription within seven (7) days after the trial expires, all Customer Data associated with the trial is permanently deleted.

3. Restrictions on Customer Data. Customer must not intentionally submit to the Offering (a) passwords, credentials or security answers in unencrypted or plain form; or (b) special categories of personal data (as defined in UK/EU GDPR) or criminal-offence data. Publisher is not responsible for consequences arising from Customer's breach of this clause.

4. Data Return and Deletion. On termination or expiry of a paid subscription, Publisher retains Customer Data for thirty (30) days, during which Customer may request in writing an export of the Customer Data it requires; Publisher will provide it in a commonly used electronic format. After that period Customer Data is permanently deleted from Publisher-controlled systems, except where retention is required by law. This supersedes any longer retention obligation in the Standard Contract.

5. Data Processing. Publisher processes personal data in accordance with its Data Processing Agreement, available at https://spadditions.com/data-processing-agreement (the "DPA"). The DPA supplements Section 2 of the Standard Contract. Customer consents to the subprocessors listed in the DPA; changes under Section 2.3(d) are notified as set out in the DPA.

6. Changes to the Offering. Publisher may add, modify or remove Offering features, provided no change materially degrades the core functionality of the Offering during a paid subscription term. Updates may be applied automatically.

7. Support; No SLA. Publisher provides support on a commercially reasonable efforts basis as described on Publisher's website. No service-level commitments or service credits apply unless expressly stated in the Order.

8. Limitation of Liability. Section 8 of the Standard Contract is amended so that each cap on Publisher's aggregate liability is the total fees paid or payable by Customer for the Offering in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the incident giving rise to the claim (or two times that amount for breach of Publisher's security obligations under Section 2.4 (Security)); the fixed monetary floors (US$500,000 and US$2,000,000) do not apply. All other provisions of Section 8, including its exclusions and exceptions, are unchanged.

9. Governing Law. Section 11.10 (Applicable law) of the Standard Contract is replaced as follows: this Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.

10. Refund. The refund under Section 10.5 of the Standard Contract is limited to the fees paid by Customer for the Offering during the twelve (12) months preceding termination.

11. Training. Publisher's anti-corruption training obligation under Section 6.1(e) is satisfied by training proportionate to Publisher's size, structure and risk profile.
